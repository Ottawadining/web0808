Chef Mitch Lacombe to Leave Gitanes After Seven Years, Pascal Bérubé Expected to Take Over

Lacombe is preparing to launch his own venture in Ottawa's west end, while Gitanes looks toward a new chapter under its current sous-chef.

the short version
  • Chef Mitch Lacombe leaves Gitanes after seven years. His final day is November 1.
  • He's planning his own venture in Ottawa's west end. No name, location or opening date yet.
  • Sous-chef Pascal Bérubé is expected to take over as head chef, though Gitanes hasn't confirmed it.

A significant change is coming to one of Ottawa's best-known restaurants. Chef Mitch Lacombe is leaving Gitanes after seven years to pursue his own venture in the city's west end. Gitanes announced the news on Instagram on October 16, and his final day at the Elgin Street restaurant will be November 1.

While Gitanes has not publicly announced a successor, OttawaCulinary.com has learned that current sous-chef Pascal Bérubé is expected to take over as head chef.

Seven Years at Gitanes

Lacombe has been a central figure in the development of Gitanes, helping establish it as a prominent destination in Ottawa's dining scene. During his tenure, the restaurant earned recognition from Canada's 100 Best Restaurants, and he contributed to its success in back-to-back sandwich competitions.

His next project will be in Ottawa's west end, where he grew up; the restaurant's announcement names Stittsville, his hometown. Its name, location, culinary direction and opening date have not yet been made public, but an established downtown chef opening in the western suburbs is a noteworthy development for that part of the city.

Pascal Bérubé Expected to Lead the Kitchen

Promoting Bérubé from within would give Gitanes continuity, with an existing member of the team stepping into the top role rather than an outside hire. The restaurant has not yet formally announced the appointment or said whether the menu's direction will change.

A Farewell Celebration on October 29

Before Lacombe's departure, Gitanes will host a farewell celebration on its patio on October 29, with barbecue from Meatings, the Ottawa-based barbecue and catering company.

For Ottawa diners, the transition brings two developments worth watching: the next chapter of Gitanes under new culinary leadership, and a new project from one of the chefs who helped shape its reputation.
