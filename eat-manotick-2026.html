At E.A.T. Manotick, the kids the money is for are cooking too

A new tasting fundraiser this Sunday puts village restaurants, city favourites and youth cooking programs at the same tables.

the short version
  • E.A.T. (Enjoy, Appreciate, Together) runs Sunday, October 25, 5 to 7 p.m., at 3700 Prince of Wales Drive in Manotick.
  • About 20 businesses are named so far, from Heist and Angelina's to Lola's Kitchen and Masala Trail, plus youth programs like Kitchen Brigades.
  • Tickets are $125, on sale until October 24. Money raised goes to Impact Forward, a new charity funding programs for young people.

Six weeks after Taste of Manotick filled Main Street, the village gets a second food night, and this one has a single beneficiary. E.A.T. stands for Enjoy, Appreciate, Together. It runs Sunday, October 25, from 5 to 7 p.m. at 3700 Prince of Wales Drive.

A village and city lineup

The format is a walk-around tasting with cooking demos and a chance to meet the people behind the food. The lineup mixes Manotick regulars with businesses from across Ottawa. From the village come Heist, Angelina's, Manotick Natural Market and Manotick's Finest. From across the city come Lola's Kitchen, Stittsville's Masala Trail, Yasmin Syrian Cooking Ottawa, the Marlborough Pub, Take Another Bite and Sip Social. Osgoode's No Go Coffee is bringing its truck.

Also on the list, per Impact Forward: Baccanalle, Garvin BBQ, G Burger, Nutrition Blocs, SharecuterieGo and Stream Water.

The tables that explain the night

Some of the tables explain why the night exists. Kitchen Brigades, La Tablée des Chefs' program, teaches teenagers to cook through their schools. R.I.S.E. is taking part too, as is Wave's Open Door Kitchen. Their presence makes the cause concrete.

The evening raises money for Impact Forward, a new registered charity whose board includes a psychotherapist, a school founder and a longtime educator. It doesn't run programs itself. It raises money for groups that offer young people mentorship, life-skills training, bursaries and mental-health support.

General admission is $125 through the event's ticket page, and sales close Saturday, October 24.
